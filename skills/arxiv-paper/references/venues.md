## Integrity

USENIX treats simultaneous submission, previously published work and plagiarism as dishonesty or fraud, with sanctions such as barring authors from its conferences [usenix-policy].

## Anonymization checklist (design, from the rows above)

- Author names and affiliations removed, including PDF metadata and acknowledgements.
- Self-citations in the third person.
- OSDI: a system name different from any public name, including your arXiv version and repository [osdi27-cfp].
- Links to code go to an anonymized copy or are left out until acceptance [neurips-checklist].
- An arXiv version is allowed at MLSys, NeurIPS and OSDI under the rules above [mlsys27-cfp][neurips26-handbook][osdi27-cfp].
