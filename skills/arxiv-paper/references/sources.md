# Sources

Use to look up a `[key]` cited in any reference file, to judge how a source was read, or to pick a classic writing guide.

Every fact in this skill comes from the learning guide "arXiv paper writing for ML systems research" (generated 2026-10-08 from 80 sources). The `Guide` column is the guide's own source id. `Read as`: `raw` = page or markdown read directly; `PDF` = PDF pages read as images; `extracted` = fetched page passed through a small-model extraction, with numbers re-checked where they matter; `snippet` = search-result snippet only, the page was not read. Treat `snippet` facts as leads, not rules.

Text marked (design) is judgement from the guide or this skill, not a sourced rule.

## Which writing guide for what

| Guide | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `[peyton-jones]` | One idea, narrative, intro, contributions, evidence | Slides; related work goes later |
| `[widom]` | The five intro questions, contributions, terminology | Short |
| `[ernst]` | Style rules, figures, precision, revision | Concrete |
| `[levin-redell]` | What systems reviewers ask | 1983 and still the reviewer's checklist |
| `[zhang]` | Thesis "X is better for Y in Z", design and evaluation structure | 2021, systems-specific |
| `[roscoe]` | The reviewer's view | 2007 |
| `[heiser]`, `[hoefler-belli]` | Evaluation honesty and statistics | Read before writing the evaluation |
| `[mensh-kording]`, `[nanda]` | Structure; ML narrative; claims | Nanda is opinionated, 2025 |
| `[gopen-swan]`, `[orwell]`, `[economist]`, `[strunk]`, `[zinsser]` | Sentence-level clarity | Classic |
| `[rougier]`, `[wilke-captions]`, `[wilke-color]` | Figures, captions, tables, color | |

## Unverified and gaps (as of 2026-10-08)

- MLSys 2027: the CFP says more submission details are coming. The CFP (20:00 UTC) and the home page (12:00 PM PDT, which is 19:00 UTC) disagree by an hour on the deadline. 2027 artifact dates were not published. The 2027 text reuses the `mlsys2025style` file and the 10-page limit.
- OSDI '27 artifact evaluation was not stated in the fetched CFP. OSDI artifact facts are from the OSDI '26 pages.
- NeurIPS 2026 handbook details (page count wording, extra camera-ready page, ZIP size) come from extraction. The checklist text itself was read raw.
- The Economist style guide pages were blocked; the 2013 archived introduction was used. The ACM badging page returned 403; badge definitions come from the EuroSys and SIGIR copies of v1.1.
- Ernst's tense rule and the arXiv help pages S1, S12 to S16 and S19 to S24 were read through extraction. The rules that matter for a submission (endorsement, rate limit, replacement, TeX, TeX Live, moderation, format) were re-read from the raw arxiv-docs markdown or the blog posts.
- Hoefler and Belli: rules 1 to 5 were read; rules 6 to 12 were not.
- Mytkowicz et al., Feldt and Magazinius, and Zenodo's `CITATION.cff` support are snippet-level only.
- No official arXiv guidance on cs.LG versus cs.DC for ML systems exists in the sources. The category advice is judgement.
- arXiv endorsement domains per category were not looked up. The submission form shows the domain.
- Not covered: venue camera-ready, copyright forms and ACM or USENIX templates, USENIX Security and SIGMOD artifact rules, posters and talks, journal submission.
